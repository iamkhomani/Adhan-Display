===== s-yunus
Yunus in the darkness

Swallowed by a great fish, in layers of darkness, Prophet Yunus did not despair. He turned to his Lord, admitted his mistake and called out for help. Allah rescued him, and promised the same rescue to every believer who calls on Him like that.

“And (mention) Dhu'n-Nun, when he went off in anger and deemed that We had no power over him, but he cried out in the darkness, saying: There is no Allah save Thee. Be Thou Glorified! Lo! I have been a wrong-doer. Then we heard his prayer and saved him from the anguish. Thus we save believers.”
— Qur'an 21:87-88 (Al-Anbiyaa), translation by M. Pickthall

===== s-musa-mother
The promise to Musa's mother

Fearing for her newborn, Musa's mother placed him in a chest on the river, trusting Allah's promise. The river carried him to Pharaoh's household, who took him in, and Allah brought him back to his mother to nurse him.

“So We restored him to his mother that she might be comforted and not grieve, and that she might know that the promise of Allah is true. But most of them know not.”

===== s-yusuf
Yusuf forgives his brothers

Thrown into a well by his own brothers, sold, wrongly imprisoned, Prophet Yusuf rose to high office in Egypt. When his brothers finally stood before him, he did not take revenge.

“He said: Have no fear this day! May Allah forgive you, and He is the Most Merciful of those who show mercy.”
— Qur'an 12:92 (Yusuf), translation by M. Pickthall
